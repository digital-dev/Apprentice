Your mix

Effect map

Glowing circles are your current effects. Hover an ingredient: a green arrow shows an effect that will turn into another one. Effects with no arrow stay the same.

🗺️

Every effect has a spot on a map

The game places all 35 effects as circles on a hidden 2D map. Your product's effects are points on that map. Open the Mix Simulator to see it.

➡️

Ingredients push effects around

Each ingredient pushes every effect on the product the same direction. If an effect lands in another effect's circle, it turns into that one. Then the ingredient adds its own effect, if there's room (max ).

💰

Effects raise the price

Each effect adds a percent of the base price, and higher tiers add more. Order matters, so always add ingredients in exactly the order shown.
